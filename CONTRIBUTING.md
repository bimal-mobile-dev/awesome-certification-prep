# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list more useful. Quality beats quantity here.

## What gets accepted
- Free resources from the certification issuer (exam outlines, official practice, free courses)
- Free primary sources the exams draw on (standards, frameworks, official guides)
- Free, reputable hands-on labs or tools
- Fixes for broken links and outdated exam codes (please link the official announcement)

## What doesn't
- Exam dumps, "real exam questions" or anything that breaks an issuer's candidate agreement
- Affiliate, referral or tracking links
- Paid products, unless clearly marked as paid and widely recognised as useful
- Self-promotion without clear value to learners

## Format
Add one line in the right section, in this format:

```
- [Resource name](https://link) - One short sentence on why it helps.
```

Keep descriptions short, factual and free of marketing language.

## How
1. Open an issue with the link and why it belongs, or
2. Edit `README.md` and open a pull request.
