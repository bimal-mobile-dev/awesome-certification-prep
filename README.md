# Awesome Certification Prep

> A curated list of **free and official** resources for preparing for professional IT, cloud, security, project, finance and HR certifications.

Most people over-buy prep material and under-use the free, official sources that the exams are actually written from. This list starts with those: the issuer's exam outline, its free learning and practice, and the frameworks the questions draw on.

No exam dumps. No "real exam questions". No affiliate links.

## How to use this list

1. **Start with the official outline.** Download the exam outline or objectives from the issuer. It lists every topic and how much each one weighs. Use it as your checklist.
2. **Find your gaps before you study.** A short diagnostic tells you which domains to focus on, so you don't spend weeks on what you already know.
3. **Learn from primary sources.** Use the issuer's free learning and the frameworks the exam draws on (NIST, the Scrum Guide, Well-Architected, and so on).
4. **Practise, then review every answer.** For each practice question, explain why the right answer is right and why each wrong answer is wrong.
5. **Re-check your readiness before you book.** Exams are expensive. Book when your weakest domain is no longer weak.

## Cybersecurity

Security+, CISSP, CISM, CCSP, CEH and related certifications.

### Official exam outlines

- [CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v7/) - Current exam page with downloadable objectives.
- [CompTIA Security+ V8 (SY0-801)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v8/) - Next version, expected around Nov 17, 2026. Preview objectives are on this page.
- [ISC2 CISSP exam outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp/cissp-certification-exam-outline) - The 8 domains and their weights. Read this before buying any book.
- [ISC2 certifications](https://www.isc2.org/certifications) - Outlines for CC, SSCP, CCSP and the rest of the ISC2 family.
- [ISACA CISM](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cism) - Job practice areas, eligibility and exam details.

### Free frameworks the exams draw on

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) - Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Shows up across CISSP, CISM and Security+.
- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) - The common web application risks, in plain language.
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) - How real attackers operate, tactic by tactic. Useful for CySA+ and SOC roles.
- [NIST glossary](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary) - Settle any "which definition is right?" question with the official wording.

## Cloud

### Official exam guides

- [AWS Certification](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/) - Every AWS exam page links its exam guide (domains, weights, in-scope services).

- [Google Cloud certification](https://cloud.google.com/learn/certification) - Exam guides for Associate and Professional certs.
- [HashiCorp certifications](https://developer.hashicorp.com/certifications) - Terraform Associate objectives and review guides.

### Free official learning and practice

- [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws/) - Free digital courses and official practice question sets for many AWS exams.

- [Azure Well-Architected](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/well-architected/) - Core reading for AZ-305.
- [Google Cloud Architecture Framework](https://cloud.google.com/architecture/framework) - Design principles behind the Professional Cloud Architect exam.

## What this list does not include

- **Exam dumps or "actual exam questions".** Using them breaks the candidate agreement you sign with the issuer and can get your certification revoked. They also teach you to memorise answers rather than understand the topic.
- **Paid courses presented as required.** Paid material can help, but nothing here assumes you buy it.
- **Affiliate or referral links.**
